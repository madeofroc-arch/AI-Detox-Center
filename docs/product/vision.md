# Vision

**AI Detox Center** (product name: **Human Mode**) is an open-source app for
AI dependency management and independent thinking training.

> Detoxing from AI is not about not using AI — it is about not letting AI
> replace your brain.

**Positioning (EN):** AI should augment human thinking, not replace it.

## The problem

AI assistants make it effortless to skip thinking. The failure mode is not
"too much AI time" — it is a *behavioral* shift:

```
Question -> AI -> Copy -> Next Question
```

People stop attempting, stop deciding, stop writing, stop remembering — not
because they can't, but because the path of least resistance never asks them
to. Existing tools respond with blocking and abstinence, which misdiagnoses
the problem: AI usage quantity is not the issue; **unconscious dependency
behavior** is.

## The solution

Human Mode trains a different loop:

```
Think -> Try -> Get Stuck -> Hint -> Think Again -> Solve -> Reflect
```

Three pillars:

1. **AI Dependency Management** — an honest, transparent, deterministic
   AI Dependency Score built from *how* you use AI (attempted first?
   delegated a decision? sought reassurance?), never from raw usage time.
   An AI Gate inserts a moment of intention before reaching for AI.
2. **Independent Thinking Training** — daily Human Challenges across nine
   capabilities (Thinking, Creativity, Writing, Memory, Decision Making,
   Problem Solving, Communication, Learning, Focus).
3. **Reflection** — brief, private, local-only reflection that converts
   experience into awareness.

## Core principles (bind every skill, doc, and line of code)

1. The goal is not to eliminate AI.
2. The goal is to eliminate unconscious dependence on AI.
3. AI should augment human intelligence, not replace human thinking.
4. The best outcome is that users eventually need the app less.
5. Privacy comes before personalization.
6. Local-first by default.
7. No shame-based design.
8. No manipulative engagement loops.
9. No fear-based messaging.
10. The product should train independence, not create another dependency.

## What Human Mode is not

- Not a Screen Time blocker.
- Not an AI prohibition or abstinence tool.
- Not a medical or clinical product; it makes no health claims.
- Not an engagement machine: success is users needing it *less*.

## Success, in one sentence

A user who, three months in, notices they pause, think, and attempt before
asking AI — and opens this app less than they used to.
